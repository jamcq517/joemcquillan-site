How I Work

I sell the way I'd want to be sold to.

Real discovery, not a script — the relationship matters more than any single deal. My approach is built on the Sandler methodology, but that's just mechanics. What drives it: doing right by the people I work with, full stop.

Genuine over transactional

I'd rather be a real friend and partner than a vendor contact in someone's inbox — high-touch and caring, not just when there's a deal on the table.

Trust before transaction

I aim to be who a client's leadership calls before the project's even defined — because they trust the recommendation to be honest, not convenient.

Discovery before pitching

I want to understand an organization's actual goals and constraints before I talk about solutions.

Speaking both languages

I'm not an engineer or architect, but I've spent years bridging business and technology — across sales, service, marketing, and now AI and data strategy — translating problems into solutions everyone understands.

Partnering, not just selling

Some of my best results come from working directly alongside Salesforce's own teams — co-selling so the client gets one coordinated recommendation, not competing pitches.

Playing the long game

Account growth and retention matter more to me than any single deal. They only happen by staying engaged — a trusted, reliable resource long after the first sale — because as trust grows, so does the partnership.
